Wambely Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Wambely ("Wambely," "we," "us," or "our") provides a mobile and web application for international money transfers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have regarding your information when you use the Wambely app and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide to us

  • Account information: full name, email address, phone number, password (stored only in encrypted/hashed form).
  • Identity verification (KYC) information: date of birth, nationality, residential/billing address, government-issued identification documents (e.g. passport, national ID, driver's license), and a selfie photo used to verify your identity, as required by financial regulations and our payment partners.
  • Financial and transaction information: recipient details (name, bank account number, routing/IFSC/SWIFT codes, bank name), transfer amounts, currencies, and transaction history.
  • Communications: information you provide when you contact customer support.

1.2 Information we collect automatically

  • Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and general log/diagnostic data.
  • Session information: login timestamps, device identifiers used to manage your active sessions, and authentication tokens.

1.3 Information from third parties

  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a unique identifier from Google to create or access your account.
  • Payment and compliance partners: our payment infrastructure partner (currently Nium) may provide us with verification results, compliance status, and transaction status updates related to your transfers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account, and authenticate you when you log in;
  • Verify your identity and comply with Know Your Customer (KYC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and other legal and regulatory obligations;
  • Process and complete your money transfers, including currency conversion and payout to your chosen recipient;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, unauthorized transactions, and other illegal activity;
  • Send you transactional communications (e.g. one-time passcodes, transfer confirmations, security alerts) and, where you have opted in, service updates and promotional communications;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Improve and maintain the security and functionality of the Service.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

  • Payment and compliance partners: we share the information necessary to process your transfer and verify your identity with our licensed payment infrastructure partner(s) (currently Nium), who are themselves regulated financial institutions bound by their own compliance and data protection obligations.
  • Service providers: we use third-party providers to operate the Service, including email delivery (currently Brevo, for sending verification codes and notifications) and sign-in services (Google, if you choose to use Google Sign-In). These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for other purposes.
  • Legal and regulatory disclosures: we may disclose information to regulators, law enforcement, or other authorities where required by law, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Business transfers: if Wambely is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy or a policy with materially similar protections.

4. International Data Transfers

Because Wambely facilitates cross-border money transfers, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own, including the jurisdictions in which our payment infrastructure partners operate. Where required by applicable law, we take steps to ensure such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as necessary to provide the Service. We also retain identity verification and transaction records for the period required by applicable financial regulations (which, for AML/KYC records, is commonly five years or more after the end of our relationship with you), even after you close your account.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information (you can update your name, email, and phone number directly in the app's profile settings);
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal obligation to retain certain financial records;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent for optional communications (e.g. rate alerts, promotions) in the app's notification preferences;
  • Request a copy of your information in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and share, and the right to request deletion, as described above. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted storage of passwords, encrypted network connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access controls restricting who within our organization and our partners can access your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a child without parental consent, we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
